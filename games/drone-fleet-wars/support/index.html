Drone Fleet Wars — Support

Bumblewing Games · contact: ashemez@outlook.com

FAQ

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Everything works offline. Game Center leaderboards sync when you are online and signed in, but nothing in the game waits for a connection.

Where is my progress stored?

On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do the power-up charges work?

You start with 2 Recon, 2 Cluster, and 2 Decoy charges. Earn more by completing daily targets, finishing in the weekly leaderboard top 3 (podium prize packs), or watching an optional rewarded ad. Charges are never sold — the game has no purchases.

How does the 10-second turn clock work?

Idle time only. Firing resets it; letting it hit zero forfeits the turn.

Sound or music not playing?

Check the SOUND and MUSIC toggles in SETTINGS (the gear button on the home screen), and the device mute switch.

Found a bug or have a suggestion?

Email ashemez@outlook.com with your device model and what happened. We read everything.
