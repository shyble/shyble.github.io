FAQ

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Everything works offline. Game Center leaderboards sync when you are online and signed in, but nothing in the game waits for a connection.

Where is my progress stored?

On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do the power-up charges work?

You start with 2 Recon, 2 Cluster, and 2 Armor charges. Earn more by completing daily targets, finishing in the daily race top 3 (podium prize packs), or watching an optional rewarded ad. Armor can also be bought with credits earned in battle. Charges and credits are never sold for real money — the game has no purchases.

What is armor?

An Armor charge plates your flagship before battle: the first enemy hit anywhere on it bounces off, and the plate is spent. A deflected shot does not destroy the cell — strike the marked cell again to finish the job. The SIEGE and ARMADA duel enemies plate their own flagship too.

What are credits?

Every finished battle pays credits — wins and harder enemies pay more, and even a loss pays a small consolation. Spend them in the ARSENAL on Armor plates, or top up with an optional rewarded ad. Credits are earned in play, never bought.

How does the 10-second turn clock work?

Idle time only. Firing resets it; letting it hit zero forfeits the turn.

Sound or music not playing?

Check the SOUND and MUSIC toggles in SETTINGS (the gear button on the home screen), and the device mute switch.

Found a bug or have a suggestion?

Email kursatkutlu@live.com with your device model and what happened. We read everything.
