Tactical strike & seek duels · by Bumblewing Games

Deploy a fleet of kamikaze drones, hide it well, and start guessing — because an enemy commander is guessing right back. Every strike you land reveals a little of their formation; every miss tells them something about yours. Read the board, chain your hits, and send their flagship down first.

What's in the game

Five ways to fight. Quick Duel for instant matches, a Campaign across ground, naval, and air theaters, Ranked duels for rating, Timed War against the clock, and Supply Raid wave runs.

Power-ups that matter. Recon sweeps peek at the enemy grid, Cluster strikes hit wide, and Decoys bait the enemy into wasting shots on a fake.

A career to climb. Daily targets, career rank, weekly leaderboard races with podium prizes, and Game Center leaderboards and achievements.

Plays anywhere. Fully playable offline. No account, no purchases — optional rewarded ads only, always your choice.
