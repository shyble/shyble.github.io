Privacy Policy — Drone Fleet Wars

Effective: 2026-08-16 · Drone Fleet Wars (Shyble)

What stays on your device. Game progress, scores, settings, your call sign, and your avatar choice are stored locally on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them. The game has no accounts and no servers of its own, and is fully playable offline.

Advertising. Drone Fleet Wars shows optional, rewarded video ads (you choose to watch them in exchange for in-game bonuses) served by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource Ltd., a Unity company). To deliver and measure ads, the ad SDK collects device information such as device identifiers (e.g. IDFV), IP address, device model and OS version, and ad interaction data. We serve contextual (non-personalized) ads and do not use the App Tracking Transparency framework to track you across other companies' apps. The ad provider processes this data under the Unity Privacy Policy.

Game Center (optional). If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, scores and achievements are submitted to Apple so leaderboards work, under the Apple Privacy Policy. The game is fully playable signed out.

Children. The game is not directed to children under 13. It contains only mild cartoon combat, no purchases, and no social features.

Changes. If a future update changes any of the above, this policy and the App Store privacy labels will be updated before that version ships.
