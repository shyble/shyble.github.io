Hearth Siege

Hold the last fire through the Long Night

The Narts have ridden to war beyond the mountains, and one by one the valley's hearths are going dark. Only Setenei still keeps hers. Chint raiders and Inyzh giants are coming down the roads to take back the fire Sosruquo once stole from them, and every road ends at her hearth.

What's in the game

Four ancestor shrines. Raise and upgrade them beside the road. Sosruquo's fire burns whole packs up close, Pataraz's arrows reach a little further, Setenei's mist slows every raider inside it, and Adiyukh's light makes each kill pay more gold.

A hearth that fights back. Whatever slips past the shrines meets the hearth itself. Strengthen it with the gold the raiders drop, and choose carefully where the next shrine stands.

Champions and Nart powers. Every fifth wave a champion leads the raid, and the last one is the strongest. Answer with the heroes' own powers once you have earned them: Sosruquo's meteor, Setenei's mist, and a volley from every arrow shrine.

Every night makes the next one stronger. Embers from each telling feed the Rite of the Names, a tree of lasting blessings for your shrines, your hearth, and the heroes who answer the call.

A weekly night for everyone. Each week the same road and the same night for every player, ranked on a weekly leaderboard.

The roads

Five roads and ninety levels down the valley, each one told again and harder.
